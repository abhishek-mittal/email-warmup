## Original defect

Each resume called the scheduler, which queued a full day's volume again, incremented `warmup_day`, and gave already-passed slots a delay of zero. Every API replica ran the cron.

## What changed

- **One schedule row per inbox per UTC day** (`warmup_schedules`, unique on inbox, date, policy version). Creating it is the only thing that advances `warmup_day`.
- **Allowance** = planned volume minus sends that are planned, submitting, accepted or uncertain. Scheduling runs in a transaction holding a row lock on the inbox; `(schedule_id, slot_index)` uniqueness backs it up.
- **Resume** on an active inbox does nothing. Pause releases reservations; resume tops up to the cap with future slots only.
- **Slots** are drawn at random across the remaining window with a guaranteed 8-minute minimum gap, never in the past, capped to what fits.
- **Pairing** picks all partners for the day in one pass and spreads them: at most 3 per shared partner, 10 per private-pool inbox, 40 inbound per receiver per day. Shared pairing requires consent on both sides, an active receiver and an active non-quarantined membership.
- One inbox failing no longer stops the rest of the cron run.

Files: `backend/src/warmup/{warmup.service,pairing.service,slot-planner}.ts`.

## Behaviour covered by tests

Single run; repeat runs; three concurrent schedulers across two service instances; five resume clicks; pause then resume with three already sent; late-day start (3 slots at 17:30); extreme jitter at both bounds with a 150/day target; non-active statuses; one failing inbox; partner spread; eligibility filters; missing sender consent; receiver capacity across 16 senders; private pool preferred; expired reservations released.

## Not done

- Multi-day staging schedule ledger (the task's done-evidence).
- Midnight and DST cases: the day boundary is UTC by rule, so DST does not apply; a run straddling 00:00 UTC is not separately tested.
- Reply traffic is not counted against sender or receiver capacity.
- **Decision needed (volume cap):** the 08:00 to 18:00 UTC window holds 76 sends at 8-minute spacing. The previous code let high days run past 18:00. Ramp targets above 76 (fast from day 15, medium at day 35) are now capped. Either accept the cap, widen the window, or shorten the spacing.
- **Decision needed (caps):** the per-pair and per-receiver numbers above are my defaults, not a product decision.
