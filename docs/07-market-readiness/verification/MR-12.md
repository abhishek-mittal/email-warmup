## Before

Nothing in the backend read bounces. The PRD requires a pause when more than 3% of an inbox's sends bounce in 24 hours. There was no way to stop sending short of stopping the process.

## What changed

**Evidence.** Each send row holds at most one bounce (`bounced_at`, `bounce_type`, `bounce_detail`). It is set from:

- a permanent rejection during SMTP submission (5xx to the envelope or message). Authentication and connection failures are not bounces.
- a delivery status notification found in the sender's own mailbox. `BounceMonitorService` scans every 15 minutes for inboxes that sent in the last three days.

**A notice counts only if** it names a Message-ID this system generated, that message was sent from the mailbox the notice arrived in, and, when the notice names a failed recipient, it is the recipient we sent to. Matched notices are marked read and filed to WarmupHub. Anything else is left untouched.

**The rule.** Rate = hard bounces / sends a worker actually attempted, over the last 24 hours by send time. Strictly more than 3% pauses the inbox with reason `bounce_rate`. Small samples are not exempt; `GET /inboxes/:id/bounce-stats` returns the counts behind the figure.

**Hold.** A `bounce_rate` pause releases reserved sends, drains queued jobs, and is not lifted by the owner's Resume. An operator releases it with `POST /internal/safety/inboxes/:id/release`; the owner can then resume.

**Stop switches.** `safety_stops` rows with scope `global`, `provider` or `user`. Checked by the scheduler before reserving and by the send worker immediately before submitting, so queued sends are covered. Replies are held back; opening and filing continue. Rows are never deleted and record who activated and cleared them.

Operator routes (all need `X-Internal-Secret`; `X-Operator` is recorded):

| Route | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET /internal/safety/stops` | Active stops; `?all=true` for history |
| `POST /internal/safety/stops` | `{ scope, key?, reason }` |
| `POST /internal/safety/stops/:id/clear` | Clear one stop |
| `POST /internal/safety/inboxes/:id/release` | Lift a bounce hold |

Files: `backend/src/safety/*`, `backend/src/warmup/{warmup-send.processor,warmup-receive.processor,warmup.service,smtp-outcome}.ts`, `backend/src/inbox-control/*`.

## Behaviour covered by tests

3 of 100 does not pause, 4 of 100 does; 1 of 2 pauses and reports the sample; zero sends; sends older than 24 hours, reservations and canceled sends excluded; temporary failures recorded but not counted; notice recorded, filed and pausing; three duplicate notices and a second scan count once; unknown Message-ID, another inbox's Message-ID, wrong recipient and the owner's own bounce all ignored and left in place; delay notice upgraded by a later permanent failure; SMTP 550 counted immediately and not retried; SMTP 535 not a bounce; resume refused under hold, allowed after release; job in flight when the pause lands sends nothing; global, provider and user stops; replies held; idempotent activation, validation, audit trail; operator routes refuse a missing, wrong or same-length secret.

Live on the dev stack: operator route without secret 401; same route through the browser proxy 404.

## A defect this work exposed

An error raised while preparing the SMTP connection (token refresh, host policy) was classified `uncertain`, which would have left such sends permanently unretried. It is now a definite failure.

## Migration and rollback

`0007_bounce_and_safety_stops.sql`: three nullable columns on `warmup_sends`, new `safety_stops`. Rollback: drop them.

## Not done

- No notification when an inbox is paused for bounces. The notify worker has no template for it (MR-18). The inbox page shows the reason.
- Complaint (feedback loop) reports are not read.
- Bounces of warmup replies are filed but not counted against the replying inbox.
- Bounce notices from real Gmail and Outlook were not tested; their wording varies. The parser reads standard fields and common free-text codes.
- No operator UI; the routes are called with curl.
- No separate circuit breaker for a provider outage; an outage shows as failed sends, not bounces.
- Rule 12's dependency on notification dedupe (MR-18) is open.
