## Reproduction of the original defect

A nodemailer `sendMail` with no `to`, against a real SMTP server, fails with `EENVELOPE No recipients defined`. That is what every warmup reply did, after the open and star flags were already set and before filing or any database write. Replies were attempted on about 60% of receive jobs.

## What changed

- **Ledger.** Each send is a `warmup_sends` row written before SMTP: `planned → submitting → accepted | failed | uncertain`, or `canceled`. The claim is a conditional update, so a job delivered twice sends once.
- **Uncertain is never resent.** A lost acknowledgement, or a worker that died mid-submission, marks the send `uncertain`. The receive job then looks for the message in the partner mailbox; finding it sets `accepted`.
- **Replies** go to the address recorded for the original sender, with a deterministic Message-ID, `In-Reply-To`, `References` and the warmup header. The reply has its own submitting/accepted/uncertain/failed state. A second job files the reply out of the original sender's inbox.
- **Receive actions** are saved one at a time as each succeeds and skipped on retry. Messages are addressed by UID under a mailbox lock; work for one mailbox is serialized in-process.
- **Rescue** depends on where this message was found, not on the previous send.
- **Retries.** Receive jobs retry with backoff for about 10.5 hours when the message has not arrived. Send jobs retry only definite rejections.
- **Recovery sweep** every 5 minutes republishes receive jobs lost between acceptance and enqueue, marks stuck submissions uncertain, and releases reservations whose slot passed.
- **Eligibility** is rechecked immediately before sending and before engaging.

Files: `backend/src/warmup/{warmup-send.processor,warmup-receive.processor,warmup-ledger.service,smtp-outcome}.ts`, `backend/src/db/schema.ts`.

## Behaviour covered by tests

## Migration and rollback

`0005_warmup_delivery_ledger.sql`: additive columns on `warmup_sends`, new `warmup_schedules`, unique indexes on `message_id`, `delivery_key` and `(schedule_id, slot_index)`. Rollback: drop those indexes, columns and the table. Existing rows default to `accepted`. The `message_id` unique index fails to build if duplicates exist; the local dev database had none.

## Not done

- Controlled real two-mailbox exchange (the task's done-evidence). Needs two real mailboxes on different domains.
- Gmail-specific behaviour (labels, `[Gmail]` container, All Mail) is handled in code but exercised only against GreenMail.
- Per-mailbox serialization is per process. Across replicas, safety rests on UIDs and re-location, not a shared lock.
- Uncertain sends are reconciled through the receiver mailbox only, not the sender's Sent folder.
- **Decision needed (filing promise):** incoming warmup mail is visible in the receiver's inbox until its engagement job runs (2 to 240 minutes). A strict "never appears" promise needs provider-side filters or revised wording.
