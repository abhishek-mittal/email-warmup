## Before

No blocklist check scored 30 of 30. No placement test scored 20 of 40. An inbox with every DNS record failing and nothing else measured scored 50. Graduation passed with no placement test, and one score row counted as a seven-day average.

## What changed

**Score, rule version 2.** Each component reports points earned and points that could be judged from real, recent data. The score is earned over judged, 0 to 100. `completeness` (0 to 100) is how many of the 100 points were judged. Nothing measured means no score row.

| Component | Max | Counts as known when |
|---|---|---|
| DNS | 30 | Check under 3 days old; each non-null record |
| Blocklists | 30 | Check under 2 days old; in proportion to lists that answered; a listing uses the full 30 |
| Placement | 40 | Complete or partial test under 35 days old; in proportion to seeds observed |

Drop alerts are skipped when completeness changed by more than 15 points between the two scores.

**Graduation, rule version 3** (threshold raised to 80 by owner decision, 2026-10-03). Minimum warmup days as before, plus: scores on at least 7 different days in the last 10, each with completeness of 60 or more; average 80 or more; a placement result under 14 days old with spam at or below 5%. Missing, pending, failed or stale data does not pass.

Dashboard shows "Based on N% of signals" under the score.

Migration `0009_score_completeness.sql`: two nullable columns. Rollback: drop them.

## Consequences

- **No inbox can graduate until seed mailboxes exist**, because a placement result is required.
- Scores recorded before this change have no completeness and do not count toward graduation.
- Existing scores will shift; the first score under the new rules is not comparable to the old one.

## AI diagnostics hardening (2026-10-04 update)

`backend/src/diagnostics/ai-analyzer.service.ts` (model pinned `claude-haiku-4-5`
per project config):

- **Real cancellation + timeout.** The call now passes an `AbortController` signal
  and the SDK `timeout` option, so a stalled request is aborted (not just the
  awaiting promise). Any failure still falls back to issue-codes-only (returns null).
- **Cost budget.** A per-process sliding window caps AI calls per minute
  (`ANTHROPIC_DIAGNOSTIC_MAX_CALLS_PER_MIN`, default 30); over budget skips the
  call and falls back.
- **Strict output validation.** Strips ```json fences / surrounding prose; drops
  causes without a valid `priority` enum or non-empty text; requires at least one
  well-formed fix; clamps `estimatedRecoveryDays` to [0, 90]; caps causes/fixes at 10.
- **Evidence + uncertainty.** Output carries `generatedAt`, an `evidence` block with
  the DNS/blacklist/placement measurement timestamps, and `confidence`
  (`high` only when a placement observation backed it). Deterministic issue codes
  remain primary and separate (`diagnostics.service.deriveIssueCodes`).

Tests: `ai-analyzer.service.spec.ts` (16) incl. fence parsing, enum drop, clamp,
confidence, cost-budget cutoff, abort-based timeout. Backend tsc clean, 616 unit
pass, build clean.
